*Noaman Khan,Nihad Hadj Sahraoui,Samir Brahim Belhaouari*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 提出一种广义分形叉积（FCP），通过非线性径向变形实现任意（可为非整数）正齐次度 D，保留经典叉积的多项关键性质，在图像融合任务中提升医学图像分类性能。

  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 经典广义欧几里得叉积的齐次度由整数维决定，限制了其在分形或非整数维结构建模中的应用；作者旨在构建一个可调齐次度、适用于自相似结构且保留关键代数性质的广义叉积形式。

Method: 定义一种具有指定正齐次度 D 的非线性径向变形，构造标量和向量形式的分形叉积（FCP）；在自相似框架下满足尺度平衡律；结合可微无量纲图像响应与经验累积指数，并将其嵌入多描述符融合框架用于医学图像分析。

Result: 在Sierpiński三角形实验中准确恢复理论分形维1.5849625；在多个医学图像数据集上，FCP融合框架显著提升AUC、平衡准确率及F1调和均值等指标。

Conclusion: 所提出的FCP有效扩展了叉积概念至非整数维情形，保留重要几何性质，并在实际图像任务中展现应用价值，但其独立贡献需进一步剥离验证。

Abstract: The magnitude of the generalized Euclidean cross product is a Gram volume whose degree under common scaling is fixed by the integer dimension of the spanning frame. We formulate a generalized Fractal Cross Product (FCP) as a nonlinear radial deformation with a prescribed positive degree $D$, which may be non-integer. The scalar construction applies in any ambient dimension $m\geq k$, while its canonically oriented vector form requires codimension one. It recovers the classical generalized cross product exactly at $D=k$ and retains orthogonality, alternation, rotation equivariance, and $D$-homogeneity, but is generally not multilinear. For exact self-similar frame systems, the construction also obeys a scale-balance law at the similarity dimension. We derive a differentiable, dimensionless image response and a non-circular empirical accumulation exponent obtained by regressing raw angular Gram responses across patch widths. Binary64 calculations recover the finite-frame identities to roundoff, while raster experiments recover the Sierpiński-triangle value 1.5849625 at three resolutions. On FracAtlas, balanced accuracy increased from 0.6186 to 0.6721 and the harmonic mean of precision and sensitivity from 0.3669 to 0.4457. These results support the utility of the complete fusion framework, but do not isolate the effect of FCP from that of its complementary descriptors and fusion head.
