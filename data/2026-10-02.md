### [1] [STATERA: Hidden Mass Estimation via Zero-Shot Sim-to-Real Kinematics using Frozen Temporal Tubelets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00003)
*Animesh Varma*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 本文提出STATERA方法，利用冻结大部分参数的预训练视频模型（V-JEPA）和轻量时序模块，从单目短视频中估计不透明、非对称刚体的质心位置，并构建HiddenMass基准数据集。实验表明该方法在仿真和零样本迁移到真实场景中显著优于基线，能更好地捕捉隐藏物理属性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有基于外观预训练的视觉模型难以从运动中推断隐藏的物理属性（如质心），尤其在存在自遮挡、表面线索和点跟踪不可靠的情况下。因此需要一种能有效利用运动信息估计质心的新方法。

Method: 提出STATERA框架：以预训练视频骨干网络V-JEPA为基础，冻结大部分权重，附加轻量级时序tubelet mixer，预测每帧质心热图与轨迹；同时构建包含50K MuJoCo轨迹和63段真实视频的HiddenMass基准。

Result: 在仿真中，STATERA-50K-Sigma将归一化质心误差从41.7%（DINOv2）降至25.2%；在零样本仿真到真实迁移中，相位感知版本STATERA-50K-Crescent虽有轻微欧氏误差增加，但物理捕捉能力从2.6%提升至41.0%，且唯一展现出向真实质心偏移的一致运动趋势。

Conclusion: 冻结的时序表征有助于将惯性动力学与视觉几何解耦，从而更有效地估计隐藏物理参数（如质心），为从视觉推断不可见物理属性提供了新思路。

Abstract: Vision models pretrained for frame-level appearance often struggle to infer hidden physical properties from motion. We study center-of-mass (CoM) localization for opaque, asymmetric rigid bodies from short monocular videos, where surface cues and point tracking are unreliable under self-occlusion. We propose STATERA, which adapts a pretrained video backbone (V-JEPA) with mostly frozen weights and a lightweight temporal tubelet mixer to predict per-frame CoM heatmaps and trajectories. To support this task, we introduce the HiddenMass Benchmark, comprising 50K MuJoCo trajectories and a 63-sequence real-world test set with physically calibrated CoM ground truth. In simulation, STATERA-50K-Sigma improves normalized CoM error from 41.7% (DINOv2) to 25.2%. In zero-shot sim-to-real transfer, we observe a fundamental trade-off in supervision: phase-aware targets can induce bimodal predictions, while phase-agnostic targets can collapse toward statistically safe centroids. Nevertheless, our phase-aware STATERA-50K-Crescent is the only evaluated method that demonstrates consistent movement toward the true hidden offset. While this leads to a monocular vector overshoot artifact that marginally increases absolute Euclidean error compared to a static geometric centroid, it improves physics capture from 2.6% to 41.0%. These results suggest that frozen temporal representations can better separate inertial dynamics from visual geometry for hidden-parameter estimation.

### [2] [Emergent Object Binding Has a Finite Spatial Horizon](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.00006)
*Mayank Singal*

Main category: cs.CV

TL;DR: 预训练视觉Transformer能编码图像块是否属于同一物体，该绑定信号具有局部性且随距离指数衰减，揭示了其表征的内在结构。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 探究预训练视觉Transformer中是否以及如何自发形成物体绑定（object binding）信号，并理解该信号的结构性质而非仅依赖整体准确率。

Method: 通过冻结的patch嵌入使用多种探针（probe）在ADE20K和COCO数据集上解码IsSameObject信号，分析绑定概率随空间距离的变化规律，并控制混淆变量以验证不同行为。

Result: 发现绑定信号具有局部性，随patch间距离呈指数衰减并趋于非零基线；该现象在不同模型（DINO、CLIP）、数据集和探针下一致；绑定对大物体较弱、同类物体区分较差、能正确关联部件与整体，但不受遮挡影响（控制物体大小后）。

Conclusion: 物体绑定是预训练视觉表征中一种具有有限空间范围的局部相干性，其结构性质解释了整体准确率无法揭示的多种行为，表明绑定是表征本身的属性。

Abstract: Pretrained Vision Transformers encode whether two image patches belong to the same object. This IsSameObject signal is decodable from frozen patch embeddings at high accuracy, which suggests that object binding emerges from self-supervised pretraining alone. We show that this single accuracy number hides the structure of the signal. Binding is local: the probability that two patches of the same object are decoded as bound falls off monotonically with the distance between them and levels off at a nonzero floor, a falloff well described by an exponential with a finite length scale. This decay holds across object sizes, across three families of probe, on both ADE20K and COCO, and across DINO and CLIP backbones, which indicates that it is a property of the representation rather than of the decoder. Reading binding as local spatial coherence with a finite range accounts for a set of behaviors that the aggregate score leaves unexplained: binding weakens on large objects, separates distinct objects of the same class less reliably than objects of different classes, and groups object parts with their wholes. It is, by contrast, unaffected by occlusion once object size is controlled. We map each behavior with confounds controlled. As a preliminary observation, the horizon and its floor are organized at different depths in DINOv2 and DINOv3, which we report as suggestive given the small number of layers probed and the confound between the two models.

Abstract (中文翻译): 预训练的视觉Transformer能够编码两个图像块是否属于同一物体。这种“是否为同一物体”（IsSameObject）信号可以从冻结的图像块嵌入中以高准确率解码出来，表明物体绑定（object binding）仅通过自监督预训练即可自发涌现。我们指出，单一的准确率数字掩盖了该信号的内在结构。绑定具有局部性：属于同一物体的两个图像块被解码为绑定的概率随着它们之间距离的增加而单调下降，并趋于一个非零的基线值，这种衰减过程可很好地用具有有限长度尺度的指数函数描述。这一衰减规律在不同物体尺寸、三类探针、ADE20K和COCO两个数据集，以及DINO和CLIP两种骨干网络中均成立，表明这是表征本身的属性，而非解码头所致。将绑定理解为具有有限范围的局部空间相干性，可以解释整体得分无法说明的一系列行为：绑定在大物体上变弱，对同类不同物体的区分不如异类物体可靠，并能将物体部件与其整体正确关联。相比之下，在控制物体大小后，遮挡并不影响绑定效果。我们通过控制混淆变量系统地刻画了每种行为。作为初步观察，DINOv2和DINOv3中该衰减的“视界”（horizon）及其基线出现在网络的不同深度，鉴于所探测层数较少且两个模型存在混淆因素，我们仅将此结果作为启发性发现予以报告。

This emergent structure facilitates dense supervision during training and enables single-forward-pass self-consistency-based quality and uncertainty estimation at test time. <details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 探究视觉语言模型中间层是否具备可解释性，特别是其中编码的正确答案信息是否对最终预测具有因果作用，以理解模型错误的根本原因。
